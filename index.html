Self-Control Task Battery

Browser-based mouse-tracking and attention-allocation tasks for cognitive research.

Task 1

Goal–Temptation Mouse-Tracking

Five two-choice trials (study goal vs. temptation). Cursor trajectories are recorded and scored with AUC / MD per Freeman & Ambady (2010), with a per-trial researcher view.
Task 2

Split-Screen: Study vs. Videos

12 minutes of free time before a quiz. Left side serves math questions (1 point each); right side plays YouTube videos. Whichever side the mouse is on becomes active. Time allocation, switches, and answers are logged.
